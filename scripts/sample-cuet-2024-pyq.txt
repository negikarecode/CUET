Q1. A parallel plate capacitor with air between the plates has a capacitance of 8 pF. What will be the capacitance if the distance between the plates is reduced by half and the space between them is filled with a substance of dielectric constant 6?
(A) 96 pF
(B) 48 pF
(C) 24 pF
(D) 12 pF
Answer: A

Q2. Assertion (A): Electrostatic field lines can never form closed loops.
Reason (R): Electrostatic fields are conservative in nature, hence work done in moving a test charge around any closed loop in an electrostatic field is zero.
(A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
(B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true but Reason (R) is NOT the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
(C) Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false.
(D) Assertion (A) is false but Reason (R) is true.
Answer: A

Q3. In the absence of a partnership deed, what is the rate of interest admissible on loan advanced by a partner to the partnership firm?
(A) 6% per annum simple interest
(B) 12% per annum simple interest
(C) 10% per annum compound interest
(D) No interest is payable on partner loan
Answer: A

Q4. Sunrise Limited purchased machinery costing ₹10,00,000 from Alpha Traders and issued 9% Debentures of ₹100 each at a discount of 10% in full satisfaction of the purchase price. How many debentures will be issued?
(A) 11,111 debentures
(B) 10,000 debentures
(C) 11,112 debentures
(D) 9,000 debentures
Answer: A

Q5. Identify the grammatically correct passive voice transformation of the sentence:
"The invigilator had distributed all the CBT question papers before the bell rang."
(A) All the CBT question papers had been distributed by the invigilator before the bell rang.
(B) All the CBT question papers have been distributed by the invigilator before the bell rang.
(C) All the CBT question papers was distributed by the invigilator before the bell rang.
(D) All the CBT question papers were being distributed by the invigilator before the bell rang.
Answer: A
